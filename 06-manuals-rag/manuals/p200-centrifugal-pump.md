# P-200 Centrifugal Pump: Operation and Maintenance Manual

Fictional equipment, written for training. Model P-200, end-suction centrifugal pump for clean water and light process fluids.

## 1 Safety

Isolate and lock out the motor before any work on the pump. Close the suction and discharge valves and drain the casing before opening it. The casing may contain fluid at up to 80 °C; allow it to cool below 40 °C before removing the cover.

Never run the pump dry. Running without fluid destroys the mechanical seal within seconds.

## 3 Installation and alignment

Mount the pump and motor on a rigid, level baseplate grouted into place. Align the coupling with a laser or dial indicator. Maximum permitted misalignment is 0.05 mm parallel offset and 0.05 mm per 100 mm angular. Recheck alignment after the first 24 hours of operation, because pipework strain and settling can move the pump.

Support suction and discharge pipework independently; the pump nozzles must not carry pipe weight.

## 4 Start-up

1. Prime the pump: fill the casing and suction line completely and vent all air through the vent plug.
2. Open the suction valve fully. Never throttle the suction side.
3. Open the discharge valve to about 25 %.
4. Start the motor and check the direction of rotation against the arrow on the casing.
5. Open the discharge valve slowly until the duty point is reached.

Do not run the pump against a closed discharge valve for more than 30 seconds; the fluid heats up quickly.

## 5 Lubrication

The bearings are oil-bath lubricated. Use mineral turbine oil of viscosity grade ISO VG 68. Keep the oil level at the centre of the sight glass when the pump is stopped.

Change the oil every 2,000 operating hours or every 6 months, whichever comes first. Change it immediately if the oil looks milky, which indicates water ingress.

## 6 Maintenance

### 6.1 Inspection schedule

Daily: check for leaks at the seal, unusual noise, and the oil level. Weekly: record bearing temperature and vibration. Every 6 months: check coupling alignment and the condition of the coupling element.

### 6.2 Mechanical seal replacement

Replace the cartridge seal as a complete unit. Do not dismantle the cartridge. Before fitting, clean the shaft sleeve and lubricate the O-rings with the grease supplied with the seal. Tighten the seal gland nuts to 25 Nm evenly. Remove the setting clips only after the gland nuts are tight.

A small amount of leakage (up to 5 drops per minute) is normal during the first hour while the seal faces bed in.

### 6.3 Bearing housing

To replace the bearings, remove the coupling half, drain the oil and withdraw the bearing housing. Heat new bearings to 110 °C with an induction heater before fitting; never use a flame. Fit the drive-end bearing (6309-C3) first.

When refitting the bearing housing cover, tighten the bearing housing bolts to 45 Nm in a cross pattern, in two stages (first 20 Nm, then 45 Nm). Fill with fresh ISO VG 68 oil.

## 7 Troubleshooting

### 7.1 High bearing temperature

Normal bearing housing temperature is up to 70 °C. The alarm is set at 85 °C and the trip at 95 °C.

Common causes: oil level too low or too high, wrong oil grade, contaminated oil, coupling misalignment, or worn bearings. Check the oil level and condition first, then alignment. If the temperature keeps rising after an oil change, inspect the bearings.

### 7.2 Low flow or low pressure

Common causes: air in the suction line, suction valve partly closed, blocked suction strainer, worn impeller or wear rings, or wrong direction of rotation. Clean the suction strainer when the pressure drop across it exceeds 0.3 bar.

## 8 Vibration limits

Measure overall vibration velocity (RMS) on both bearing housings in horizontal, vertical and axial directions.

| Condition | Velocity RMS |
|---|---|
| Good | below 2.8 mm/s |
| Alert: plan maintenance | 2.8 to 4.5 mm/s |
| Trip: stop the pump | above 4.5 mm/s |

A rising trend matters more than a single reading. An increase of 25 % between two weekly readings should be investigated even if the value is still in the good range.
