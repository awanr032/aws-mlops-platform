# C-75 Rotary Screw Compressor: Operation and Maintenance Manual

Fictional equipment, written for training. Model C-75, oil-injected rotary screw air compressor.

## 1 Safety

Stop the compressor, isolate the electrical supply and vent all internal pressure before opening any part of the air or oil system. The oil separator vessel remains under pressure after shutdown until the blow-down valve has fully vented it; wait at least 2 minutes and confirm the pressure gauge reads 0 bar.

Hot surfaces: the air end and discharge pipe can exceed 100 °C.

## 3 Daily checks

Check the oil level in the sight glass with the compressor stopped; it must be between the MIN and MAX marks. Check that the automatic condensate drain operates; press its test button and confirm water is discharged. Check the controller for warnings.

## 4 Oil and oil filter

Use only the synthetic compressor oil specified on the data plate (ISO VG 46 synthetic). Do not mix oil types.

Change the oil and the oil filter every 4,000 operating hours or once a year, whichever comes first. In ambient temperatures above 35 °C, halve the interval.

## 5 Air filter

Replace the air filter element every 2,000 operating hours, or earlier if the filter differential pressure indicator shows more than 50 mbar. Never clean and reuse paper elements. A blocked air filter reduces delivery and raises the discharge temperature.

## 6 Oil separator element

Replace the oil separator element every 4,000 operating hours, or when the differential pressure across it exceeds 1 bar. High oil carry-over into the compressed air (oil in the air lines or at the condensate drain) also indicates a failed separator element.

When fitting a new element, make sure the earthing staple in the gasket is in place, to prevent static build-up.

## 7 Drive belts

Check belt tension every 1,000 hours. Deflection should be 6 mm at the centre of the span under a force of 50 N. Replace belts as a matched set, never individually.

## 9 Troubleshooting

### 9.1 High discharge temperature

Common causes: oil level too low, blocked oil cooler fins, ambient temperature too high or poor ventilation in the compressor room, blocked oil filter, faulty thermostatic valve, or a blocked air filter. Check the oil level first, then clean the cooler fins with compressed air from the inside outwards. The compressor room must stay below 40 °C.

### 9.2 Compressor does not reach pressure

Common causes: air demand higher than compressor capacity, leaks in the distribution system, inlet valve not opening fully, or a blocked air filter. A leak test at weekends (no demand) shows how quickly the system pressure falls; a fall of more than 1 bar in 10 minutes indicates significant leaks.
